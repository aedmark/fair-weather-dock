Maritime Field Guide to Pier Guests

SIX DOCUMENTED COMPOSITIONS // OBSERVED UNDER MILD INCONVENIENCE

Specimen 01 // Corrugated Pulp

The Hype Cutout

Shouts "RIDE OR DIE" in every group chat and comments fire emojis within four seconds. Possesses the load-bearing capacity of a damp cereal box.

Diagnostic tell: Hollow thud when poked; folds flat at first sight of furniture.

Specimen 02 // Pressurized Gas

The Helium Bestie

Perpetually thrilled to see you, insists you "MUST catch up this week," and is already drifting into the upper stratosphere before choosing a Tuesday.

Diagnostic tell: Squeaks on contact; untethers immediately when plans require a calendar.

Specimen 03 // Low-Bitrate Projection

The Signal Hologram

Renders in crisp high definition whenever you are hosting dinner. Drops to 144p static the moment you stop initiating every single conversation.

Diagnostic tell: Dissolves into scanlines and leaves a floating "Seen 3w ago" receipt.

Specimen 04 // Coiled Spring

The Track-Star Bolter

Keeps marathon shoes double-knotted at all times. Clocks a sub-four-minute mile off the pier the instant an itemized restaurant check hits the table.

Diagnostic tell: Sweats visibly when your cursor nears any responsibility button.

Specimen 05 // Uncoated Stock

The Origami Sailor

Allergic to overcast skies, hard weeks, or unglamorous errands. Creases neatly into a paper boat at 62% humidity and sails smoothly out of reach.

Diagnostic tell: Refuses indoor/outdoor transitions; catches the first gust east.

Specimen 06 // Seasoned Cast-Iron

The Real One

Rare. Speaks in lowercase. Brings snacks without being asked, puts on a yellow slicker when it pours, grabs the heavy end of the sofa, and refuses to be flicked off the pier.

Diagnostic tell: Rings with a solid iron clunk; plants boots at the edge of the dock.
